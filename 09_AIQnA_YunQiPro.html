What are the clinical implications of 木不及 Mù Bù Jí (Wood Deficiency) in 2027?
YQ

In 2027 (丁未 Dīng Wèi), 木不及 Mù Bù Jí as the Dà Yùn 大運 means the Wood phase is insufficiently strong. Key clinical patterns include Liver/Gallbladder vulnerability, Earth overacting (土乘木 Tǔ Chéng Mù), and Metal counteracting (金侮木 Jīn Wǔ Mù).
