Chapter 66 — 天元紀大論

Energy Almanac · Celestial Origins

厥陰之上，風氣主之；
少陰之上，熱氣主之；
太陰之上，濕氣主之；
少陽之上，相火主之；
陽明之上，燥氣主之；
太陽之上，寒氣主之。
Above Jué Yīn, Wind Qì governs;
Above Shào Yīn, Heat Qì governs;
Above Tài Yīn, Dampness Qì governs;
Above Shào Yáng, Ministerial Fire governs;
Above Yáng Míng, Dryness Qì governs;
Above Tài Yáng, Cold Qì governs.
Commentary

This passage establishes the foundational pairing of each of the six channels with its governing atmospheric influence. Directly relevant to 2027 where 太陰濕土 appears as Double Damp in Jul–Sep segment.
